# Cashford 2.0 — IA/UX: two independent takes

## Claude's take (JTBD-first)

### The one structural insight

The World Cup app's atomic unit was **the match** — match cards, match pages, per-match money.
In gameweek format the atomic unit is **the gameweek**: you enter it, sweat it, win it, and pay for
it as a unit. Every IA decision follows from moving the center of gravity from match to gameweek.
Matches don't disappear; they demote from destination to detail.

### Member JTBDs and the surface that serves each

| # | Job | Surface |
|---|-----|---------|
| 1 | "A new gameweek is open — get my picks in before the deadline, fast, without missing a fixture." | Gameweek entry sheet (one screen, all fixtures) |
| 2 | "Games are on — how are my picks doing against my friends, right now?" | Gameweek tab, live state (GW leaderboard + per-fixture breakdown) |
| 3 | "The week's done — who took the pot and what do I owe / get?" | Gameweek tab, settled state + Dues delta |
| 4 | "Who's actually the best predictor this season?" | Season table (points, exacts, GW wins, money net) |
| 5 | "I paid Utki in person — record it so the balance is true." | Dues: log + confirm settlement |
| 6 | "Should I back Arsenal to win 2-0? What do the odds/form say?" | Insights, inline per fixture inside the entry sheet |
| 7 | "Remember the World Cup? Pull up the bracket / old dues." | Competition switcher → archived WC (rare access, zero daily clutter) |

Captain adds: invite/manage members, set stake (future GWs only), see who hasn't entered and nudge
them — via a share-to-WhatsApp reminder, since WhatsApp is this product's real notification channel.

### Navigation

**App level** — keep the current three-tab home shape but re-point it:
- **Leagues** (default): league cards showing net balance + the active competition's next deadline
  and my entry status ("GW3 · not entered · locks Fri 23:00"). The entry-status-on-card is the
  cheapest possible "do I need to act?" signal — no new Home surface needed.
- **Matches**: keep, but re-organized by gameweek (competition-scoped). It's the lightweight
  "when do games kick off / live scores" hub people open mid-weekend.
- **Analytics**: keep, scoped to competition; WC analytics freeze into the archive.
- **Bracket** nav link: removed; lives only inside the WC archive.

**League level** — replace Next 24h / Later / Live / Done / Dues with:
1. **Gameweek** (default, stateful): pre-deadline = entry status, countdown, pot, who's in, entry
   sheet CTA; live = GW leaderboard, everyone's picks, remaining fixtures; settled = winner, pot,
   payout, next GW's card. `‹ GW3 ›` navigator to browse past/future gameweeks.
2. **Season**: standings table + past gameweek history.
3. **Dues**: unchanged in spirit + settlement logging. Sits ABOVE competitions — one balance per
   league across WC + PL. No competition switcher here, ever.
- **Competition switcher** in the league header: PL 2026-27 (active) / World Cup 2026 (archived,
  read-only — its old tabs incl. bracket preserved as they were).

### Entry flow

One scrollable sheet, all ~10 fixtures, grouped by day. Score steppers per row; progress indicator
("8/10 picked"); deadline pinned while scrolling; explicit submit that states the ante ("Entering
stakes ₹100"). Insights expand inline under each fixture row — never a page hop mid-entry. Edit
until deadline; withdraw is a distinct, confirmed action (it removes your stake). Cross-league
mirroring stays at submit time, per-league consent with each league's stake shown.

### Match detail page

Survives as a drill-in from the gameweek board: live board, everyone's picks post-lock, what-if,
full insights. Prediction editing moves OUT of it — one submission surface (the entry sheet), or
edits fork silently.

### Scope guard (Aug 21)

Ship first: gameweek tab (3 states) + entry sheet + season table + dues with settlement logging +
competition switcher/archive. Polish later: what-if scenarios on the GW board, richer analytics,
share-reminder card, entry-status badges everywhere.

## Where Sol and Claude agree (adopt without further debate)

1. Kill the time-bucket tabs (Next 24h/Later/Live/Done); one stateful gameweek screen, default at
   every point in the weekly cycle — state changes in place, users never get moved.
2. Entry is a single sheet with day grouping, progress count, inline expandable insights, explicit
   staked-entry confirmation, edit-until-deadline, withdraw as separate confirmed action.
3. Money/Dues is league-level, above competitions; no competition switcher on it; every row names
   its source ("PL GW8 pot"); settlement confirmation is first-class.
4. WC archive (incl. bracket) lives behind the competition chooser, read-only; old public bracket
   links keep working; bracket leaves daily nav.
5. Match detail demotes to secondary drill-in; never a second submission surface. Cross-league
   copying requires per-league consent + stake display.

## Where we differ (decide these)

| # | Question | Sol | Claude | Stakes |
|---|----------|-----|--------|--------|
| D1 | App-level nav | Kill Matches AND Analytics; new **Home = action queue** (needs-attention cards) + Leagues + Account | Keep the 3-tab home; entry-status on league cards does the "act now" job; Matches stays as GW-organized live hub | Sol's is the cleaner end-state but builds a new surface; Claude's reuses what exists. Affects 4-week scope directly |
| D2 | Tab naming | League tabs **This GW / Season / Money**; "Dues" sounds like a bill; cups relabel to "This round" | **Gameweek / Season / Dues**; "Dues" is established with real users | Pure naming; user familiarity vs clarity for newcomers |
| D3 | Draft state | Explicit three-state model: Not entered / **Draft** (saved picks, no stake) / Entered | Two states: not entered / entered (picks held client-side until submit) | Draft = safer (half-done picks survive devices) but adds a state users must understand ("I drafted but never entered — why did I lose?" is the exact failure opt-in was meant to avoid) |
| D4 | Season tab depth | Four sub-sections: Cashford table / Gameweeks / Stats / Fixtures & results (absorbs Matches + Analytics) | Season = table + GW history; fixtures stay in app-level Matches; analytics stays app-level | Consequence of D1 — decide together |
| D5 | Edit semantics | Edit opens a **working copy**; previous entry stands until replacement saved; deadline mid-edit → last submitted stands | Same instinct, but simple in-place editing with autosave may be fine at 5–15 users | Sol's is strictly safer; costs a draft/versions mechanism |

### Resolved by Ananth, 2026-07-26

- **D1 → Claude.** Keep the 3-tab home (Leagues / Matches / Analytics); league cards gain
  entry-status badges ("GW3 · not entered · locks Fri 23:00"). No new Home surface.
- **D2 → Claude.** League tabs: **Gameweek / Season / Dues**. Cups relabel the first tab later.
- **D3 → two states (overrules both recommendations).** Not entered / Entered. Picks live
  client-side until an explicit Enter, which is the single staking action. No server-side drafts.
- **D4 → follows D1.** Matches stays app-level, reorganized by gameweek. Analytics stays
  app-level, competition-scoped. Season tab = standings + gameweek history only.
- **D5 → default.** Entered picks edit in place with an explicit save; deadline passing with
  unsaved changes means the last saved entry stands. Unsaved changes never count.

## Sol's brief-gaps both of us endorse

Inline explanations for awkward states (void fixture, blank/double GW, one-entrant refund,
re-settlement) rather than a rules-page trip; archived competitions keep the rules text that
applied then; deadline shown as absolute time + countdown (IST), not countdown alone; the
share-to-WhatsApp deadline reminder as the product's notification strategy.
