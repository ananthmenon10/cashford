Eight throwaway sketches of how a player reaches the gameweek that's on now and the one that just finished. Four for the league page, four for the home page. Everything uses the live cs2 tokens; toggle your OS theme to check dark.

Sample state: GW 3 live (Sat–Mon), GW 2 settled (you won ₹200 in Solid Yenne Boys), GW 4 opens Tuesday. What's current today: ‹ GW 3 › arrows on home; ?gw= tabs on the league page.
