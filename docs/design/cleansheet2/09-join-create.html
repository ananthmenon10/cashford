GW1 and GW2 aren't yours to answer for

A joiner scrolling back finds "Before your time" and who won, not an empty entry sheet or a zero. Their dues start at ₹0 and their season total starts at GW3, so the season table can say "3 of 3 entered" rather than "3 of 5" against someone who wasn't there.

Cases & flows

Creating
  • Two fields, two consequences. The ante is followed by what it does — everyone stakes it, the highest score takes the pot — and by the rule that changing it later only touches future gameweeks.
  • The competition is confirmed, not chosen. One line with a "change" link. Only competitions running now appear; an archived competition can be read but never started.
  • The last thing the flow says is a deadline. "First gameweek: GW3 · deadline Fri 5 Sep, 23:00 IST" — absolute, with the timezone, because that's the commitment being made.
  • No ante is set per gameweek at creation. One number for the league; per-gameweek overrides would make dues unreadable.
Joining
  • Preview before the tap: competition, ante, member count, next deadline. Nobody joins blind.
  • Joining is free. Said once, next to the button, because the ante is the thing people hesitate over.
  • The invite message is shown, not just sent. Link plus a plain code, since links get mangled in group chats.
  • A stale code gets its own answer on this screen rather than a toast: the league exists, it just isn't taking members.
Arriving mid-season
  • Earlier gameweeks read "Before your time" with the winner named. No entry sheet, no zero, no "you missed this".
  • Dues open at ₹0 and the season total counts from the gameweek you joined.
  • Entered counts are out of what you could have entered — "3 of 3", not "3 of 5".
  • The current gameweek is normal. If it's still open you can enter it on day one, at the same ante as everyone else.
