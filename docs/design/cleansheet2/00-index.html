Cashford 2.0 — the whole product

Cashford is a prediction and settle-up game for a WhatsApp group. From August 2026 the unit is the gameweek, not the match: predict every fixture before one deadline to enter, stake the league ante, and the highest score takes that week's pot. Ties break on exacts, then on total goal miss. Leagues are permanent and carry one continuous balance — the World Cup archives read-only behind a competition switcher. Money never moves through the app; Dues records the payments people actually make and both parties confirm them.

Pass 2 (27 Jul): entry is final so withdraw is gone everywhere, entering is one tap with no confirm sheet, mirroring shows only when another league has the same competition running, leagues gain a Table tab, the Matches tab collapses every league into one card and gains the real league table, the World Cup archive leads with Analytics and gives the bracket its own tab, and joining or creating a league finally has screens.

Design system & later skins

Clean Sheet 2.0 — one system throughout
  • Type: Hanken Grotesk for text, Geist Mono with tabular figures for every numeral, score, point total and rupee amount, so columns line up and scores never jitter.
  • Colour has meaning. Quiet green is money and progress only. Amber is a deadline or a state needing attention. Red appears only for a dispute or a negative balance. Everything else is ink, paper and hairline dividers.
  • Money is always ₹ and always mono. Deadlines are always absolute local time plus a countdown, never a countdown alone.
  • Awkward states explain themselves in place — void fixture, blank gameweek, one-entrant refund, re-settlement — rather than sending anyone to a rules page.
  • Soft shadows only on things that float: the confirm sheets and the sticky call-to-action bar. Cards sit on hairlines.
Later skins (same data, same flows)
  • Broadcast — for the live surfaces only: Matches during play, the gameweek race, match detail. Bigger scores, darker chrome, minute-by-minute emphasis.
  • Quant Desk — for Analytics only: denser tables, small multiples, more numbers per square inch for the people who actually read them.
  • Both stay per-screen toggles back to Clean Sheet, which remains the default everywhere. No skin changes a rule, a label, or a number.
