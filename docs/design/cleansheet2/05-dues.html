Disputed payment and a reversal

A dispute parks the record outside the balance rather than deleting it. A mistake on a confirmed record produces a visible reversal pair, so the history reads as an audit trail — the same treatment a re-settled gameweek gets.

Cases & flows

  • Two-party records only. A settlement names a payer, a receiver, an exact amount and a date. It never "zeroes the league" and never touches a third person's balance.
  • Either party can log it; the other confirms. States are pending → confirmed / disputed. Confirmed is immutable; corrections are reversal entries.
  • Partial payments allowed. The amount recorded is what was entered, not "whatever clears the debt" — a gameweek can settle while the sheet is open and the typed number still stands.
  • Overpayment warns, never blocks. Someone paying ahead is a real thing in a friend group.
  • No competition switcher on Dues. One league, one currency (INR), one continuous balance across the World Cup and the Premier League. Archived competitions keep their entries immutable while the balance stays open forever.
  • Every activity row names its source — "PL GW8 pot", "PL GW7 ante", "WC Final", "payment received" — so a number can always be traced to the thing that caused it.
  • Departed members keep their balance and stay in the feed; they are deactivated rather than removed.
  • Cashford records, it does not move money. The payments happen over UPI or in cash; this screen only agrees on what happened.
