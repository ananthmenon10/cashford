The bracket, on its own

Its own tab rather than a section buried in Matches. The circle stays exactly as it was, old shared links still open it, and beneath it sits the only Cashford question the bracket ever answered: which rounds you called right and what they were worth.

Cases & flows

  • The switcher never appears on Dues. Money is league-level and spans competitions; offering a filter there would imply two balances.
  • Archived competitions are fully read-only — no entry, no editing, no new payouts. Their transfers stay immutable while the league balance stays open forever.
  • Archives keep their own rules text and their own tab set. The World Cup keeps per-match stakes and the bracket; the Premier League gets Gameweek / Season / Dues.
  • Old share links keep working. Public bracket URLs shared in the group chat in July 2026 still open the same page.
  • Analytics for an archived competition freeze at the final settled state and are excluded from current-season form.
  • The competition choice persists per league across visits, so nobody lands in the archive by accident, and app-level Matches and Analytics carry their own switcher with the same rules.
  • A member who joined after the World Cup sees the archive with a "you weren't in this one" note rather than empty rows.
