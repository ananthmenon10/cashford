The table exists from the first settlement but says so plainly. Non-entrants read as grey zeroes with a note, so an empty row is never mistaken for a bad week.

Cases & flows

  • Sitting out costs nothing and scores nothing. Points count entered gameweeks only, so the table rewards turning up as well as predicting well — and the screen states that rather than leaving it to be inferred.
  • Never-entered members appear with grey zeroes and no money, not with a losing balance.
  • Void gameweeks (one entrant) show in history with the ante returned and contribute nothing to points or money.
  • Blank and double gameweeks are labelled in history with their fixture count, because a 6-point win in a 4-fixture week needs the context.
  • Re-settled gameweeks carry the date of the correction; the row's money reflects the reversal plus the new payout.
  • Departed members stay in history and in the standings they earned; they are deactivated, never deleted, and their balance survives.
  • Season scope is one competition. The switcher changes the table; the Dues balance underneath it does not, because money is league-level.
  • No season-end money in v1 — the table is bragging rights, every pot is weekly.
