The competition table, inside the league

Added as a fourth tab because people check the table while they are already looking at their league, and sending them out to the Matches tab to do it is a detour. It is literally the same component — no Cashford numbers, no ranks of your own, just the competition as it stands.

Cases & flows

Which gameweek shows
  • Current-GW rule: a locked-but-unsettled gameweek stays current until it settles. On settlement the screen flips to the next open gameweek and keeps a compact "GW3 settled" recap strip that links back.
  • No live gameweek → the next one with a future deadline. Nothing published ahead → the latest settled gameweek. Two locked gameweeks overlapping (a postponement edge) → the newer is current and the older carries an alert.
  • The ‹ GW3 › navigator reaches past and published-future gameweeks; future fixture lists are marked "subject to change" because they follow the source until lock.
Entry and edits
  • Two states only: not entered / entered. Predicting every fixture and pressing Enter is the single staking action; there is no saved draft to lose.
  • Deadline passes mid-edit → the last saved entry stands. Unsaved changes never count, and the screen says so when it locks.
  • There is no withdraw. Entering commits the ante — that is what makes the pot real to everyone else in it. The picks stay editable until the deadline, which is the flexibility people actually want.
  • Late joiner enters from the next open gameweek at a zero balance; past gameweeks show as not entered, not as losses.
Scoring and money
  • 3 points for an exact scoreline, 1 point for the right result only — exact replaces the result point, it does not stack. Summed over the gameweek's counted fixtures.
  • Ties: most exacts → lowest total goal miss → split the pot in whole rupees. The live table shows those columns before they matter.
  • Void fixture (postponed out, before or after its kickoff time) counts for nobody and becomes predictable again in its new gameweek. An abandoned match counts only the competition's official final result; a full replay voids the original.
  • Payouts land in Dues as ordinary two-party movements; the league balance is one continuous number across competitions.
  • Stake changes apply to future gameweeks only, so an open pot can never change size under the people already in it.
