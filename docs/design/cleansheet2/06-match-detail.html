Cases & flows

One scroll per state, fixed order
  • No tabs. Pre: header → your calls → the room → insights → predicted XI. Live: header → your points → what-if → the room → match stats. Full time: verdict → your calls → the room → retrospective → post-match modules. The same fact is always in the same place, so the page can be read at a glance in a group chat argument.
  • Score is a dash before kickoff, never 0-0.
  • Kickoff and deadline are separate lines pre-match, because they are usually different days and only one of them takes picks.
Picks and the room
  • No single-fixture editing, ever. The only edit route is "Edit all picks", which opens that league's gameweek entry sheet. One submission surface, or picks fork.
  • Reveal at the gameweek deadline. Before it: entrant names plus locked placeholders. After it: every entrant's scoreline for every fixture.
  • Your own calls show for all your leagues here, each with its ante, because they can differ — and when they do, each one scores on its own.
  • The room is sorted by value. Live, that means what each call is worth at the current score. At full time it is verdict, then "Off by".
  • A viewer without access to a league follows the same deep link to the neutral fixture, with insights and no friend picks.
Sources, freshness and absence
  • ESPN is the backbone: score and events every 60 seconds, stats every 2 minutes, odds tightening from 6-hourly to every 10 minutes near kickoff. Every module carries the age of the data behind it.
  • FotMob runs on a four-hourly jittered poll and only for what ESPN lacks: match xG and the shot map, player ratings and Player of the Match, momentum, and predicted lineups when they happen to be there.
  • Nothing FotMob-fed appears live. A four-hourly source cannot describe a match in progress, so the live frame carries five stats and says where the rest went.
  • Absent beats empty. If a poll returns nothing or the shape changes, the module does not render. There is no skeleton, no zero, no "data unavailable" row.
  • Predicted XI is opportunistic. It shows only when the poll caught one and is fresh; ESPN's confirmed lineups replace it about 75 minutes before kickoff.
Edges
  • Void fixture: the page keeps insights and picks for the record and states "void in GW3 — counts for nothing, predictable again in GW14".
  • Corrected result: the verdict and the board re-score with a dated note. Nothing is edited silently.
  • No odds for a fixture: the row is left out rather than filled with zeroes, and the model's top scores stand alone.
  • Deep links carry competition, gameweek, fixture and optionally a league, and keep working after the competition archives.
