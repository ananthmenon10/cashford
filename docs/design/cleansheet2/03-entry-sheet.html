In, and asked once about the other leagues

There is no withdraw — anywhere. Entry is the one irreversible act and the caption says so on every screen that follows it. The mirror prompt only renders when you are in another league with this competition running, and the grey line states that condition out loud so the pattern is legible in review. Each copy names that league's ante, because a silent mirror would stake money nobody agreed to.

Cases & flows

Two states, no draft, no withdraw
  • Not entered / entered. Picks live on the device until Enter, which is the single staking action. There is deliberately no server-side draft, because "I drafted but never entered" is the exact confusion opt-in was meant to remove.
  • Entry is final. Once you are in, the ₹100 is in the pot and stays there. Picks keep editing in place until the deadline. Withdrawal was cut on 27 Jul 2026: it let people watch the entrant count and duck out, which is the one thing a friend-group pot cannot survive.
  • All 10 fixtures or nothing. The CTA counts what is missing ("2 more to go") and only becomes the staking button at 10/10.
  • Deadline passes mid-edit → the last saved entry stands. Unsaved changes never count, and the sheet says so as it locks.
Filling the sheet
  • Chips are the primary input. Top three model scorelines with probabilities on every collapsed row; one tap fills and advances. Steppers stay for the other 40% of calls.
  • Never default 0-0. Empty renders as a dot so an intentional 0-0 is distinguishable from an untouched row.
  • "Fill from the model" fills only the empty rows and tags each one model; the tag disappears the moment you change that score, so you can always see which calls are yours.
  • "Copy last week's scorelines" maps by club, not by row, and leaves any fixture without a match from last week empty.
  • Insights expand under the row — odds, form, head-to-head, team news — and end with a "Detailed match insight" link that is Coming soon until the match-detail rebuild ships.
Money and mirroring
  • Every league is its own entry. The mirror prompt appears only if you belong to another league with this competition active; otherwise nothing renders. It asks per league and names that league's ante.
  • A copy is independent from the moment it is made and can be edited on its own.
  • Ante changes are future-only, so a stake can never move under an open pot.
Timing edges
  • Late joiner opens straight into the next open gameweek; earlier gameweeks read "before your time" rather than showing as missed.
  • Fixture list follows the source until the deadline, then freezes. A fixture leaving the gameweek beforehand disappears and the count drops with it (10/10 becomes 9/9).
  • Blank gameweek: fewer rows, same ante, sheet works identically.
  • Insights can be stale — every panel carries its own age stamp, so nobody reads two-hour-old odds as live.
