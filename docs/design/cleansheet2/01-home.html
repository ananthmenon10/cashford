Cases & flows

Entry status
  • "Entered" at app level = entered in ≥1 league for that gameweek, always shown as a count ("entered in 1 of 3"). There is never a partial third state.
  • Entry CTAs from app tabs route to a named league's entry sheet and ask which league when it is ambiguous. No global edit exists, because it could silently flatten picks that differ per league.
  • Badge states on the league card: amber "not entered · locks Fri 23:00 IST", green "entered ✓ · edit until…", green pulse "GW live · you 7 pts · P2". After settlement the badge becomes the winner recap until the next gameweek opens.
Matches tab
  • Current-GW rule: a locked-but-unsettled gameweek stays current until it settles; then focus flips to the next open gameweek with a "GW3 settled" recap strip. No live gameweek → next one with a future deadline. Nothing published → latest settled.
  • Live money is provisional and labelled as such, per entered league, with an in-progress mark. Settled gameweeks show actual net.
  • Picks reveal at the gameweek deadline — all of them, including Sunday's fixtures on Friday night. Before that, entrant names show and scorelines never do.
  • Void fixture: postponed out of the gameweek → dashed row, "void in GW3", counts toward nothing, predictable again in its new gameweek.
  • Historical gameweek chosen by hand persists for the visit; the next visit returns to current. Future fixtures are marked "subject to change".
Analytics tab
  • Frozen during live play. Cards hold at the last settled gameweek with a live strip linking to the race. No provisional analytics anywhere.
  • Money only from settled contexts — pot won in the "GW added" card, season net in My form. Debt lives in Dues, never here.
  • Competition choice persists across visits; the view always resets to My form rather than a previous friend comparison.
  • Never merges leagues. Rates aggregate across leagues; points, pots and comparisons are always under one named league.
